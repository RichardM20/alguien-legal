Requests, agreements, and payments

  1. Posting a request, expressing interest, or opening a chat does not confirm a job or guarantee a reply.
  2. Before accepting, agree on what will be done, when, and at what price. Any payment you agree on takes place outside Alguien. The app does not receive or hold that money.
  3. Both people confirm completion. Reviews depend on the interaction and whether the conditions for leaving one are met.

A request expires after 24 hours. Distances are approximate and do not guarantee that anyone is available.

Posts and conduct

Describe clearly what you need or offer. Only share text and photos you have the right to publish. Do not use Alguien to deceive, harass, threaten, impersonate others, or disclose private information without permission.

Chat safety

Use chat to coordinate your request or service. Alguien does not ask for passwords, verification codes, card details, photos of identity documents, or medical or intimate information in chat. Do not send those details.

If you need to arrange a visit, share the address only when necessary and with the person you arranged it with, never in a public post.

What to do if something goes wrong

For support, email alguien.service@gmail.com.

If someone pressures you for private information, you suspect a scam, or you receive threats, do not share more data. You can block the person and report the conversation or content in Alguien. Reporting does not guarantee a penalty or automatically resolve a payment dispute.

What each party is responsible for

Each person is responsible for what they send, for having permission to share it, and for the decisions they make in their agreements. Alguien facilitates contact; it does not guarantee that another person's statements are true or that they will honor an agreement.
