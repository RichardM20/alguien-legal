Delete account

You can delete your account from Profile or request deletion by email without reinstalling Alguien.

Development draft. Retention periods are approved. Production activation and the effective date remain pending.

The effective date will be set at public launch, after verifying the deletion and retention described here. Time-based cleanup remains disabled during preparation.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Alguien and go to Profile.
  2. Select Delete account and read what happens to your history.
  3. Verify your identity in the app and confirm. You cannot undo deletion.

Request by email

If possible, write from the email address associated with your account. Say that you want to delete your Alguien account. Sending the message will not delete your account automatically: we will first verify that it belongs to you.

Request deletion by email (alguien.service@gmail.com).

Never include passwords, verification codes, or photos of identity documents in your first message.

What happens to your data

What stops being available

You lose access to your account, your profile is no longer shown, and the reviews you wrote stop contributing to visible ratings.

What may be kept

Private-data cleanup is scheduled at 30 days. Your shared messages and proposals may be kept for 6 months after account deletion under “Deleted account.” We do not automatically delete the other person's messages. Reports retain restricted evidence while open and for 12 months after closure, unless a specific, justified hold applies. See details, exceptions, and backups in the retention policy.
