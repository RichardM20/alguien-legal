Data, purpose, and visibility

Email and account
Used for registration, sign-in, and account recovery. Your sign-in email is not shown on your profile.
Profile, posts, and reviews
Your name, photo, description, and published content may be visible in Alguien depending on the feature and its visibility rules.
Location and preferences
Used to find nearby people and requests. Others may see an approximate distance, not your exact coordinates.
Interests, chats, and agreements
Used to coordinate requests or services. Participants can see messages and files they receive. Interests and offers are not published on your profile.
Blocks and reports
Used to manage unwanted interactions and investigate possible abuse. Reports and their attachments are not public.
Notification identifier
Used to send alerts when you enable notifications. It is not shown to other people.

Before sending a message

Share only what is necessary. Avoid sending passwords, verification codes, financial data, identity documents, or medical or intimate information. If you put personal data in a message, Alguien processes it as chat content and the other person can see it.

Permissions and providers

You can choose your location manually where the catalog is available. Denying camera, image access, or notifications limits the related action, not access to your account.

Alguien uses Supabase for authentication, data, and files, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications. Hosting regions and other deployment details will be confirmed before publication.

Deletion and retention

Deleting your account removes access, hides your profile, and stops reviews you wrote from counting toward visible ratings. It does not mean every record disappears immediately.

Shared history may appear under “Deleted account” to protect participants and allow possible abuse to be reviewed. We do not retain it as guaranteed legal evidence or indefinitely for that reason.

Private data: 30 days
From account deletion, cleanup is scheduled for publication content, locations no longer in use, comments from withdrawn reviews, the personal inbox, and device records. Images without references are removed through the file cleanup process.
Chat and proposals: 6 months
Measured from account deletion. Afterward, the deleted account's messages, associated files with no remaining valid reference, proposals, and request context in chats are removed. Messages written by the other person are not automatically deleted.
Reports: 12 months after closure
Open reports and evidence are retained with restricted access and flagged for review every 30 days. The 12-month period begins when a report is resolved or dismissed; reopening it starts a new period at its next closure.
Our technical records: 90 days
Periodic cleanup covers completed notification delivery records, image inspection attempts, block changes, publication creation events, and daily counters. Passwords, codes, and tokens must not be logged. Provider-managed logs follow their own terms, which must be verified before launch.

These periods are enforced by periodic batch tasks, not instantaneous deletion when a timer expires. An applicable obligation or documented dispute may require specific content to be kept longer. Each exception requires a reason, restricted access, and a review date; it does not suspend cleanup of the entire account. Pending cleanup resumes when the exception is released.

Minimal identifiers and interaction states may remain to preserve other participants' history and prevent deleted content from being restored. Replacing a name with “Deleted account” does not by itself anonymize a message. If another participant wrote personal information about you, you may request a review of that content.

Backups

The public service is not deployed yet, and we have not configured our own production backup process. The planned deployment uses Supabase Free, and we do not guarantee automatic backups or recovery. If we introduce backups, their retention will be defined and communicated before use. Database backups do not by themselves include Storage files. Deletions and pending retention decisions must be reapplied before a restored system is reopened.
